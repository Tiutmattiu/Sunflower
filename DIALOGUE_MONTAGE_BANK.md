# Sunflower — Dialogue & Montage Bank v1

> **Status: authored content bank, September 2026.**
>
> Companion to `NPC_CHARACTER_BIBLE.md` and `NARRATIVE_KARMA_MATRIX.md`.
>
> These are **state-aware scenelets and line banks**, not random barks and not a linear screenplay.
>
> They are written as original Sunflower material using high-level craft lessons from:
>
> - Richard Brautigan, *Revenge of the Lawn* — short scenelets, deadpan absurdity, hard cuts, ordinary objects carrying narrative weight;
> - Vladimir Nabokov, *Lolita* — self-description contradicted by records/actions, selective narration, charm not equal to truth;
> - Ocean Vuong, *On Earth We're Briefly Gorgeous* — material recurrence, body/labour memory, temporal montage, practical acts carrying intimacy.
>
> Do **not** copy source sentences or imitate a living author's voice.
>
> Sunflower should remain plainer, drier, more practical, and less lyrical than any of these references.

---

# 1. Performance Rules

1. Most lines should be short.
2. Characters do not announce their symbolic meaning.
3. Strange things are stated as ordinary facts.
4. Records can contradict speech without a narrator explaining the contradiction.
5. Player narration describes sensation or fact, not emotion.
6. Repeated lines are allowed when the changed context does the work.
7. A scene may end before the emotional point is explained.
8. Silence can be state-aware content.
9. A character may be wrong.
10. A character may lie.
11. A character may believe their own explanation.
12. Dialogue must obey actor knowledge.
13. No one explains the whole cosmology.
14. Romance is not a branch; repeated attention is enough.
15. These scenelets should be selected by state, not shuffled randomly.

# 17. Exclusion Rules for Dialogue Generation

Do not generate a scene simply because a character exists.

Do not let:

- Aspen reveal weather she did not check;
- Sterling know a debt hidden from the Bar;
- Yasmin know Animal-network gossip without a source;
- Wong know elite family decisions without a path;
- Juan know metaphysical truth because he is the Clown;
- Dima know every black-market action globally.

Do not turn every scene into teaching.

A scene can exist only because:

- somebody wants a drink;
- a fish smells;
- a fee is annoying;
- the weather changed;
- a debt is due;
- a toad bit someone;
- a person came back.

That is enough.

# 18. Tone Failures to Avoid

Bad:

> “Perhaps the true currency was trust.”

Bad:

> “You realise that home was inside you all along.”

Bad:

> “In this harbour, debts are just memories wearing numbers.”

Bad:

> “Aspen's watch was a tiny prison around her wrist.”

Bad:

> “Yasmin smiled the smile of a woman who had priced every soul in the room.”

Bad:

> “Dima's tracksuit shimmered like the uniform of an unofficial king.”

Bad:

> “Sterling's kindness was both a gift and a wound.”

These sentences know what they mean before the player does.

Prefer:

> Aspen checks the watch.

> Yasmin asks who introduced you.

> Dima says the fee is two.

> Sterling writes the tab.

> Wong counts the tins.

> Juan asks to try again.

Then let the world continue.
