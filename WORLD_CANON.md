## 1. North Star: One Structure, Changing Legibility

Sunflower does **not** become:

> Trading Game -> Mystery Game -> Spiritual Game.

From the first action to the last, the same structure is operating.

The recurring primitives are:

- Goods
- Cash
- Information
- Obligations
- Relationships
- Time
- Body / Form
- Access
- Attention

A Day 1 purchase can already be:

- an exchange of goods and cash;
- an information decision;
- an allocation of scarce time;
- a relationship event;
- a commitment that changes future access;
- karmic action.

Later lives / forms / records / consequences change what that same action means without making the original economic description false.

The player does not discover that the first game was fake.

The player may discover that they were already inside a structure they could only see from one position.

The trading game therefore remains intellectually real all the way down.

## 2. Opening: Sunflower / Seed / Home

The player begins with only:

> 🌻  
> **You want a sunflower.**  
> You do not know why.  
> You only know that it feels like a way home.

Do not reveal the deeper seed condition at the opening.

The sunflower is a real living object, not a fake MacGuffin and not literally "Mother".

The first major reveal remains:

1. objective: **Get a sunflower**;
2. player acquires one;
3. `You got it.`;
4. nothing metaphysical happens;
5. `Sunflower ×1` remains in inventory, unpriced;
6. objective becomes **Go home**;
7. the same world and market continue.

The flower did not lie.

The player assigned it a function it could not complete.

The player's deeper biological direction is germination / becoming rather than literal return into the acquired flower.

Exactly what `home` finally means remains open.

## 3. The Sun Moment Is the World Clock

The Sun Moment is a real organism-level / metaphysical event.

It is **not**:

- a state religion;
- a civic ritual imposed by Authority;
- a button labelled meditation;
- a late-game Juan mechanic;
- a metaphor that only appears in dialogue.

Living beings naturally enter it.

At fixed moments, the ordinary separation between goal-directed agents briefly loosens.

Production pauses.  
Negotiation pauses.  
Consumption pauses.  
Separate projects pause.

The current day structure places Sun Moments at:

- Sunrise;
- immediately before Noon public clearing;
- Sunset.

Societies can build clocks, stories, habits, research and institutions around the phenomenon.

Those interpretations do not cause it.

The event is real.  
Its ultimate explanation remains contested.

Possible in-world descriptions may include:

- biological/autonomic;
- religious/non-separation;
- practical market downtime;
- legal/administrative;
- Juan's experimental theories;
- children's untheorised experience.

No single actor is automatically the final authority.

## 4. Resistance Is Economically Real

A living being can resist the Sun Moment.

Resistance is not automatically punished.

It can create a genuine local advantage.

Examples:

- one trader keeps analysing while others pause;
- one late commitment is made while competitors disengage;
- one investigator keeps looking;
- one deadline is served while everyone else lets go.

The core loop is:

> attachment -> local advantage -> reinforcement -> harder disengagement

This must exist mechanically.

The world must never reduce it to:

> greed -> punishment.

Attachment is not identical to desire.

A person can become unable to stop because of:

- money;
- reputation;
- professional competence;
- responsibility;
- children;
- animals;
- love;
- guilt;
- safety;
- duty;
- curiosity;
- freedom;
- artistic work;
- scientific work;
- enlightenment itself.

An ambitious trader may release cleanly.

A supposedly spiritual person may be incapable of stopping the project of becoming liberated.

### No visible spiritual score

Do not show:

- Entanglement 73/100
- Karma +4
- Enlightenment progress
- "Correct: Let Go"

The engine may retain factual history:

- which Sun Moment occurred;
- what opportunity was present;
- release or resistance;
- what concrete action was taken;
- what immediate worldly consequence followed.

Do not infer spiritual correctness from that record.

### Gradual entanglement

Repeated resistance can make later disengagement more difficult.

This is gradual, not instant divine punishment.

The late expression may eventually be:

> everyone else stops; the player's market still works.

Later still, the player may notice that meaningful refusal of continued work has become harder or absent.

The exact threshold / transformation mapping remains deliberately unresolved until separately frozen.

## 5. Karma

Keep the compact line:

> **Karma is action that has not finished happening.**

But `unfinished` does not mean:

- quest incomplete;
- bill unpaid;
- dialogue arc unfinished;
- cosmic morality debt.

Technical design gloss:

> **Action continues as conditions for further action.**

A contract can be fully settled and continue karmically.

Aspen fulfils every delivery:
- people rely on her more;
- more work is routed through her;
- usefulness becomes more structurally necessary.

Wong rescues an animal:
- the rescue succeeds;
- the animal must now be fed;
- other people bring him more animals.

Juan repays a claim:
- repayment establishes credibility;
- future lenders increase exposure.

The action has finished administratively.  
It continues causally.

This is the bridge between metaphysics and simulation.

The engine should care about what action makes:

- easier;
- harder;
- expected;
- credible;
- normal;
- socially required;
- accessible;
- habitual;
- inherited.

No generic `karmaPoints` field should become gameplay authority.

## 6. The Six Primary Characters Are Karmic Recurrences

The six primary recurring figures are:

- **Aspen**
- **Sterling**
- **Yasmin**
- **Wong**
- **Juan**
- **Dima**

They are not six therapy lessons.

They are not ordinary random quest givers.

They must behave, locally, as full people with:

- their own income;
- recurring expenses;
- private utility;
- bounded knowledge;
- relationships with each other;
- business models;
- mistakes;
- agency outside the player's attention.

But their repeated presence across the player's lives is karmically meaningful.

The actions between player and character keep recreating conditions under which the relation returns.

A knot may eventually cease to recur only when the relation no longer reproduces the same causal structure.

That should be rare.

It cannot be unlocked by a single morally correct dialogue choice.

"Release" does not mean:
- NPC personality repaired;
- NPC became good;
- player completed a side quest.

Whether release means disappearance, another realm, non-recurrence with this player, or something stranger is deliberately not forced into one explanation.

### Economic functions outlive a released character

If a character no longer recurs as the player's knot, their economic function does not vanish from the universe.

There can still be:

- another Sailor;
- another broker;
- another grower;
- another capital allocator;
- another high-turnover dealer;
- another bar operator.

This is essential to an economy capable of samsaric continuation.

## 10. Rebirth / Form / Legal Identity

The player is a continuing point of view, not a permanent legal person.

Memory may persist across form changes.

Legal identity does not automatically persist.

When a legal human life ends / transforms:
- old formal property remains with the estate / old legal structure;
- old titles do not automatically follow;
- recognised contracts do not automatically reassign;
- debts owed to the old legal person remain claims of that old legal identity / estate;
- the new form cannot simply spend the old account.

This rule is central:

> **memory continuity != legal-person continuity**

The game should not solve this with a probate simulator.

Narrow workarounds can arise through:
- family recognition;
- proxy;
- trust;
- informal claims;
- Dima;
- tolerated cross-form venues;
- later institutions.

### Hunger and support

Hunger / sustenance can be one causal doorway into life transition.

Before a life collapses for lack of food, the game can test:
- cash;
- owned food;
- credit;
- relationship support;
- household/network support.

If nobody feeds or finances the player, form transition can occur.

This is an implemented/prototype expression of causal continuity, not the final universal rebirth formula.

### Rebirth should not be a random skin roll

Future transformations should be retrospectively intelligible from patterns of the life lived.

Potential causal inputs can include:
- repeated Sun Moment resistance;
- relation to property;
- dependency;
- hunger;
- mobility;
- unfinished obligations;
- repeated ways of using other beings;
- attachment structures.

Do not expose a karma score.

The traditional six realms are real conditions within the setting.

Human and Animal are already concrete parts of the world.

The other realms do not need to be exposed, labelled or fully playable at the opening.

Do not turn the six realms into:
- morality ranks;
- RPG levels;
- psychological percentage scores.

A person in one realm can exhibit structures that echo another realm; that does not erase the realm's ontological reality.

### Sailor

`Sailor` is a liminal form / social function as well as a concrete economic role.

Aspen is one current Sailor.

There can be other Sailors.

Sailors:
- cross boundaries;
- connect external markets;
- move goods / information;
- disappear and return;
- are less rooted, not automatically liberated.

The exact relationship between Sailor and the traditional six realms remains open.

### Plant

Plant is a serious Sunflower-specific form possibility.

Plant is not declared to be one of the traditional six realms.

Plant form radically changes:
- agency;
- mobility;
- time scale;
- legal status;
- who assigns value to whom.

Earlier forms ask:

> What is this worth to me?

Plant form can ask:

> Why are others assigning value to me?

The exact relationship between Plant, seed biology and final liberation remains open.

## 14. Sunflower Routes

Routes are discoveries, not a visible quest list.

They are not mutually exclusive "romance routes" or class routes.

A robust route is:

> signal -> contest -> outcome -> aftermath -> second-order opportunity

Missing one trade does not erase an entire route.

### Sonya Supper — people / non-market allocation

Relationship, food, hospitality and family continuity can move the sunflower outside ordinary pricing.

The route must not become "Grandma is the answer".

Sonya is supporting, not the secret oracle.

### Yasmin Auction — capital / private market

Private access, provenance, capital, auction structure and valuation can create a route to a sunflower.

### Juan Cliff — risk / boundary experiment

Juan's wagers, Onewheel, altered-state experiments and belief in a boundary-crossing possibility can expose a high-risk route.

Juan is not automatically correct about the cosmology.

None of these routes is the final victory.

> You got the sunflower. Nothing happens. Go home.

## 18. Diagnosis

Final player diagnosis must distinguish:

### Outcome
What happened.

### Process / Skill
How the player acted given what they could know at commitment.

### Style
Repeated preferences in how the player structured decisions.

Rules:
- Outcome != Process.
- Style != Skill.
- No evidence != average.
- One event is anecdote.
- Confidence requires independent decisions in varied contexts.
- Contradictory behaviour remains visible.
- Do not infer inner motive.

The engine may observe:
- repeated attention to a person;
- repeated use of private markets;
- repeated liquidity choices;
- repeated verification;
- repeated concentration.

It may not conclude:
- love;
- greed;
- moral worth;
- enlightenment;
- true personality essence.

The diagnosis describes behaviour under experienced conditions.  
It is not the player's "true self".

## 27. Final Design Test

If the player can enjoy Sunflower only by understanding the metaphysics, the market is too weak.

If the player can master the market while never being affected by form, time, relationship, recurrence or the Sun Moment, the world is too weak.

The target is one system.

The same action should be capable of being:

- a good trade;
- a bad trade;
- a relationship event;
- a legal event;
- a future claim;
- a remembered object;
- a karmic continuation;

depending on what actually happened.

No second hidden "real game" is waiting underneath.
