## Octopus clearing — preserve the mechanism and its status

Participants commit orders backed by their own cash and physical goods; the institution processes exchange and settlement, not a communal investment pot. Explain what is committed/locked, what actually trades, what remains owned but unavailable, and what returns or is released after resolution. Failed/unfilled commitments cannot silently consume principal; explicit agreed fees or actual physical deterioration need separate accounting. Settlement records distinguish completed transfers from released/unspent resources.
